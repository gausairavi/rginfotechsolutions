About Ravi Gauswami

Senior Software Engineer, Mobile & Enterprise Architect, and Creator of high-performance digital products that scale.

Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India

Engineering with Precision & Pragmatism

Founder at RG Infotech Solutions • Senior Software Engineer

With over 7 years of hands-on experience in software engineering, Ravi Gauswami specializes in architecting high-scale mobile applications, enterprise .NET backends, and cloud microservices. His engineering ethos emphasizes end-to-end system speed: eliminating synchronous service chains, refining database roundtrips, and creating delightful user experiences that don't make users wait.

Ravi completed his Master of Computer Applications (MCA) from the prestigious Atmiya Institute of Technology & Science (SFI), Rajkot (2017 – 2019), building on a solid computer science foundation. As a Senior Software Engineer and founder of RG Infotech Solutions, he has spearheaded the architecture, development, and release of enterprise web solutions and mobile applications like The Grand Athlete App and PR Recipe Hub (live on the Google Play Store and web at prrecipehub.com).

Core Tenets

The RG Infotech Engineering Philosophy

"System Speed Over Component Vanity"

An API endpoint can return in 20ms, but if synchronous service hops and blocking calls chain together, user experience degrades. We architect for the total journey of each request.

"The Product Chooses the Framework"

We avoid trend-chasing debates like Flutter vs React Native or Native vs Hybrid. The product's longevity, team competencies, and native integration depth determine the tool.

"Architecture & Quality from Day One"

Every mobile application and enterprise service is engineered with clean modular architecture, rigorous automated testing, encrypted storage, and high-throughput reliability.
