FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you ensure apps comply with Google Play Developer Policies?

We audit all Android permissions, prepare complete transparent privacy policies (such as those for The Grand Athlete App and PR Recipe Hub), verify Target SDK levels, and properly configure Data Safety declarations in the Google Play Console before submission.

Can we add new apps to the portfolio later?

Yes! The website architecture is modularized. Each app has its own folder under apps/<AppName>/ containing its dedicated download.html, privacy-policy.html, and assets, and is automatically featured on the central portfolio.html page.

Do you work on both mobile apps and enterprise backends?

Yes. Our core specialty is full-stack synergy: connecting native Android / Flutter mobile clients with enterprise ASP.NET Core, C#, Python, and SQL microservices for end-to-end performance.
