Data Deletion Policy & Instructions

Application: KharchKit: Expense Tracker • Publisher: RG Infotech Solutions

Zero Cloud Storage = Zero Waiting Period

Because KharchKit does not transmit or store your financial records on cloud servers, you do not need to submit a ticket, verify your email, or wait 30 days for deletion.

How to Delete Your Data

1

Method A: One-Tap Atomic Wipe Inside KharchKit

1. Open KharchKit and tap the Settings icon (bottom navigation).

2. Scroll down to the Privacy & Data section.

3. Tap "Erase All Data" and confirm the prompt.

Result: All transaction records, custom categories, budgets, recurring rules, and cached exports are instantly and permanently purged from your device storage.

2

Method B: Android System App Storage Reset

1. Open your device's Settings > Apps > KharchKit.

2. Tap Storage & Cache.

3. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data).

Result: The Android operating system immediately destroys the app sandbox directory, removing all database files and cached preferences.

3

Method C: Uninstalling the Application

Long-press the KharchKit app icon on your home screen or app drawer and tap Uninstall.

Result: Android automatically deletes the entire application package and all local databases permanently.

External Backups & Google Play Subscriptions

Exported Files: If you previously shared or saved .kbackup, CSV spreadsheets, or PDF reports to your Google Drive, SD Card, or email, deleting data inside KharchKit does not automatically remove those external files. You can delete them directly from the folders where you saved them.

KharchKit Pro Subscriptions: Deleting your local data or uninstalling the app does not automatically cancel active Google Play subscriptions. To cancel renewal, go to Google Play Store > Profile > Payments & Subscriptions > Subscriptions and cancel your KharchKit Pro subscription.
