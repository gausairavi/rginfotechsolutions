  • Equipment, Space & Environmental Safety: You are solely responsible for ensuring your training environment has sufficient clearance, level footing, and is free of slip or trip hazards. Inspect all exercise equipment (dumbbells, barbells, pull-up bars, resistance bands) for damage, wear, or instability prior to each workout.
  • Hydration, Recovery & Heat Safety: Maintain proper fluid intake before, during, and after exercise. Never train in excessive heat or unventilated spaces without acclimatization. Adhere to rest days to prevent overtraining syndrome, central nervous system fatigue, and musculoskeletal injury.
