Privacy Policy for The Grand Athlete App

Last Updated: September 2026 | Effective Date: September 2026

At RG Infotech Solutions (accessible from rginfotechsolutions.com), operated by Ravi Gauswami (Gausai), one of our main priorities is the privacy and data security of our visitors and users of The Grand Athlete App ("Application", "Service"). This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by The Grand Athlete App and how we use and protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you install and utilize The Grand Athlete App, we may collect the following categories of information to provide personal athletic analytics:

  • Health & Physical Fitness Metrics: Workout durations, distance traveled, running cadence, heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), and calculated calorie expenditures when granted permission.
  • Device & Hardware Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, network state, and crash logs to maintain stability.
  • Location Data (Optional): Precise or approximate location (GPS) solely while tracking outdoor athletic activities (such as running, cycling, or sprinting) to calculate routes and elevation changes. Background location is only accessed when you explicitly activate a background workout tracking session.
  • Account & Profile Details: If you choose to sync your workouts across devices, we collect your name, email address, and age/weight to calculate accurate metabolic rate analytics.

2. Android System Permissions Used

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy, The Grand Athlete App requests only the permissions strictly required to perform its core athletic functions:

  • android.permission.ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION: Used to detect steps, running intervals, and active workouts.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION: Used strictly to map exercise routes and accurately determine running speed.
  • android.permission.BLUETOOTH_CONNECT / BLUETOOTH_SCAN: Used to discover and communicate with external chest-strap heart rate monitors or smart sensors.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to display ongoing workout timers and recovery reminders.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information for purposes including:

  • Generating personalized conditioning and recovery scorecards;
  • Providing offline chart rendering and local workout logs;
  • Improving app responsiveness, reducing latency, and resolving software crashes;
  • Ensuring compliance with Google Play Developer and security standards.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The Application utilizes trusted third-party service providers that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics
  • Google Fit / Health Connect API

5. Data Retention, Security & Encryption

We employ industry-standard encryption (AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit). Your sensitive biometric data is stored primarily inside your device's encrypted SQLite/Room database. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data to advertisers, data brokers, or third-party marketing agencies.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR / CCPA / DPDP)

You have full ownership of your data. You may request to:

  • Export a copy of your stored training logs in CSV/JSON format directly inside the app settings;
  • Delete your workout history or permanently erase your synced account by sending an email to our data officer at privacy@rginfotechsolutions.com;
  • Revoke any device permission at any time from your Android device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

The Grand Athlete App is not targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you discover that your child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to adhere to evolving Google Play Console policies and data privacy regulations. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes.
