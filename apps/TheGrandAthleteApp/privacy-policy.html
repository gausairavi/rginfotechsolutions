Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how The Grand Athlete ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your personal data, health metrics, and device information in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, GDPR (EU/UK), and relevant data protection regulations.

🛡️ Summary: Private On-Device Data & Transparency

Your fitness is your own business: All your workouts, body weight metrics, meal plans, streaks, and personal progress records reside exclusively in encrypted local storage on your device. We do NOT operate remote user profile databases and we do NOT sell your personal health information. An active internet connection is required to run the app, stream exercise demonstration videos, and load non-intrusive advertisements in compliance with Google AdMob and GDPR / UMP consent choices.

1. Architecture & On-Device Storage Model

The Grand Athlete is built on a private on-device storage architecture. Unlike conventional fitness applications:

  • No Mandatory Account: You do not need to provide an email address, create a password, or link third-party social media accounts to use the application.
  • Local Database / Key-Value Store: Your athletic profiles, workout sets, reps, weights, calorie and water logs, streak records, and achievements are stored directly in your smartphone's internal sandboxed application storage.
  • Zero Remote Cloud Sync of Biometrics: We do not transmit your body measurements, workout history, or nutrition logs to proprietary external backend servers.

2. Network Connection Requirement

An active internet connection is required to run The Grand Athlete application. Network connectivity is utilized strictly for the following functions:

  • Exercise Video Demonstrations: Streaming high-definition exercise technique guides and movement tutorials embedded via the official YouTube player in conformity with the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Google AdMob Monetization: Serving banner, app-open, and optional rewarded advertisements delivered by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.
  • App Updates & Version Checks: Verifying Google Play Store version status for critical security and compatibility patches.

4. Subscriptions, In-App Purchases & Google Play Billing

The Grand Athlete offers optional premium features ("The Grand Athlete Pro") via auto-renewing subscriptions (Monthly, 3-Month, 6-Month, and Yearly plans):

  • 100% Google Play Billing: All financial transactions, recurring billing, and payment processing are handled exclusively through Google Play In-App Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING). We never collect, store, or have access to your credit card numbers, bank accounts, or billing addresses.
  • Entitlement Verification & Purchase History: When you purchase Pro, the app receives a secure, cryptographically signed purchase token and order identifier from Google Play to grant you instant access to premium features (including complete ad elimination, advanced analytics, and custom routines). This token is stored locally in sandboxed storage on your device.
  • Auto-Renewal Terms: All subscriptions automatically renew unless auto-renewal is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing cycle. Your Google Play account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the conclusion of the active period.
  • Cancellation & Management: You can manage, modify, or cancel your active subscriptions at any time through your Google Play Store Account Settings by navigating to Google Play Store → Profile Icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancelling ensures no future renewals will occur; you retain Pro benefits through the remainder of your pre-paid term.
  • Restore Purchases: If you reinstall the app or change devices, your active subscriptions can be instantly restored at zero additional cost by tapping "Restore Purchases" in the in-app Upgrade to Pro screen.
Terms of Service & In-App Billing Conditions: For complete subscription policies, billing rules, and terms, see our Terms of Service.

5. Google AdMob, GDPR & User Messaging Platform (UMP)

To keep core workout planning and exercise databases accessible for free, The Grand Athlete displays non-intrusive advertisements supported by Google AdMob.

Strict Workout Isolation Rule: Active workout player sessions are strictly 100% ad-free. No banner or interstitial ads are displayed during live exercise execution to protect your focus, timing, and safety.

Compliance with Google EU User Consent Policy & GDPR:

  • For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom (UK), our application integrates the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.
  • Users are presented with a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) dialog upon first launch to make informed choices regarding personalized vs. non-personalized advertisements.
  • Right to Revoke or Change Consent: In full compliance with Google and GDPR policies, you can review, modify, or revoke your consent choices at any time directly within the app by navigating to Settings → Privacy & Data Sovereignty → "Review GDPR Ad Consent Choices".
  • To learn more about how Google processes information when you use partner apps, please review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

6. Camera, Photos & AI Food & Macro Scanner Permissions

The Grand Athlete includes an optional AI Food & Macro Scanner designed to help athletes effortlessly log meals and calculate estimated nutritional intake (calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fats).

To enable this functionality, the application may request the following system permissions on your mobile device:

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Requested only when you tap the camera icon to photograph a meal plate or food item in real time.
  • Read Media Images / Storage Permission (android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Requested only when you choose to select an existing food image from your device's photo gallery.
Strict Image Privacy & Local On-Device Handling: Photos captured or selected for food scanning are used strictly and exclusively to analyze meal components and estimate macronutrients. The Grand Athlete does NOT collect facial biometrics, does NOT maintain a remote photo repository, does NOT access your broader personal photo library, and does NOT sell or share your images with advertising partners or external data brokers.

Voluntary Consent & Revocation: Granting camera and photo access is completely voluntary. You can use all core workout, exercise, and hydration features of the application without granting camera or media permissions. You can review or revoke camera and photo library permissions at any time through your Android system settings under Settings → Apps → The Grand Athlete → Permissions.

8. Health Connect & On-Device Sensors

If you choose to enable Health Connect synchronization:

  • The app requests read-only permissions for daily step counts and active calories solely to display local performance rings on your home dashboard.

9. Data Retention, Portability & Deletion Rights

Because your fitness data is stored on-device, you have absolute ownership and control over your records:

  • Export Your History: You can export your complete workout logs, body metrics, meal plans, and streak history at any time as a portable JSON document via Settings → Data Management & Backup → Export Backup.
  • Selective Cleanup: You can selectively delete specific categories of data (e.g. video watch cache, hydration history) without losing your workout templates.
  • Permanent Deletion / Clean Slate: You can irrevocably wipe all local data and reset the application at any time using the "Clean Slate / Reset All" button in Settings, or by clearing app storage in Android settings / uninstalling the app.
  • Advertising ID Deletion / Reset: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID (GAID) at any time through your Android device settings under Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID".
  • Data Deletion Assistance: For dedicated instructions or assistance with data deletion, visit our Data Deletion Instructions Page or contact info@rginfotechsolutions.com.
