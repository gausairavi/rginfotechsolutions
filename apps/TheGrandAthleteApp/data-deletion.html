Data Deletion Request & Policy

Instructions on how to view, manage, export, and permanently delete your personal health data and advertising identifiers.

Privacy-First Architecture: The Grand Athlete is built with a local-first, database-free architecture. Users do not register or maintain cloud accounts on our servers. All workouts, body metrics, calorie logs, and personal routines are stored strictly on your local device.
1
Delete All Data In-App (Instant Clean Slate)

You can instantly and permanently erase all fitness logs, saved routines, calorie history, and user preferences directly inside the application:

  • Open The Grand Athlete app.
  • Tap the Settings tab in the bottom navigation.
  • Select Data Management & Backup.
  • Tap "Clean Slate / Reset All Data" and confirm the prompt.

This action permanently wipes all local storage and returns the app to its freshly installed state.

2
Delete Data via Android Settings / Uninstall

Because all personal records are stored strictly in your device's private app sandbox, you can delete everything through your Android operating system:

  • Clear App Storage: Open Android Settings → Apps → The Grand Athlete → Storage & Cache → "Clear Storage" / "Clear Data".
  • Uninstall the App: Long-press The Grand Athlete icon on your home screen and select Uninstall. All application files, databases, and cached videos will be immediately and irrevocably erased from your device.
3
Reset or Delete Google Advertising Identifier (GAID)

To serve non-intrusive ads that keep our workout player free, Google AdMob processes your Google Advertising ID. You can reset or delete this identifier at any time through your Android device settings:

  • Open your Android phone's Settings app.
  • Navigate to Google → Ads.
  • Tap "Delete advertising ID" (or "Reset advertising ID").

Once deleted, apps will receive a string of zeros instead of your advertising identifier.
