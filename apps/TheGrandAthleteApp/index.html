Holistic Recovery & Pacing

Overtraining ruins consistency. Start each morning with a 30-second readiness assessment that evaluates your sleep quality, muscle recovery, and energy levels.

  • Prime Status (85-100%): Full green light for heavy progressive overload
  • Solid Readiness (70-84%): Proceed with planned session with standard warmup
  • Moderate (50-69%): Auto-adjust sets down by 1 or increase rest timers
  • Rest Day Alert: Recommends mobility stretching or light active walks
