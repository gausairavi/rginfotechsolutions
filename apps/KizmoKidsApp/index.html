Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents need to know about safety, privacy, content, and subscriptions.

What age group is Kizmo Kids designed for? +
Kizmo Kids is tailored specifically for toddlers, preschoolers, kindergartners, and early elementary learners aged 2 to 8 years old. The modules adapt to different skill levels—from initial sensory colors and ABC songs for toddlers up to multiplication basics and reading sentences for 7-8 year olds.
Is my child's data truly safe and private? +
Yes, completely! Kizmo Kids operates on a local-first architecture. We do not require accounts, logins, email addresses, or real names. All stars, lessons, and artwork are stored strictly inside your device’s sandboxed storage. We do not operate remote user tracking databases, and we comply strictly with COPPA, GDPR-K, and Google Play Families requirements.
Can my child play the app while offline? +
Yes! All core learning games, flashcards, drawing canvases, and educational modules run completely offline without an active Wi-Fi or cellular network connection. Perfect for airplane trips, road travel, or low-connectivity environments.
How does the Parent Gate prevent accidental purchases? +
Whenever an adult action is triggered (such as accessing settings, reviewing privacy options, or managing subscriptions), Kizmo Kids presents a dynamically generated adult mental math problem (like 17 + 8 or 14 × 3). Children cannot guess or bypass this barrier, and 3 incorrect tries triggers a temporary lockout.
Can multiple siblings use the same device? +
Yes! Parents can set up separate child profiles (using nicknames and cheerful cartoon avatars) for each child in your family. Each child has their own individual star progress, drawing portfolio, and favorite worlds saved on the device.
