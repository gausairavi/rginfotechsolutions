1. Introduction & Commitment to Child Safety

This Privacy Policy explains how Kizmo Kids: Learn, Play & Grow ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you or your child download and use our mobile application on Android devices.

We are committed to full compliance with global child protection regulations, including:

  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) (United States, 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506)
  • General Data Protection Regulation for Children (GDPR-K) (European Union, Regulation (EU) 2016/679 Art. 8)
  • Google Play Families Policy and Designed for Families Program requirements
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA) under-16 provisions
  • India Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023) Section 9 child privacy standards

2. Zero Personal Data Collection (PII)

We adhere to a strict data minimization by design philosophy. Kizmo Kids does NOT collect, request, process, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from children or parents.

  • No User Account: No registration, social sign-in, username, password, email verification, or phone number verification is requested or required.
  • No Real Names or Contact Data: Profiles created within the app only allow local nicknames (e.g., "Leo" or "Maya") and pre-illustrated cartoon avatars.
  • No Geolocation Data: The app does not request, access, or track coarse or fine GPS location.
  • No Biometric Data: The app does not collect facial recognition, fingerprint data, voiceprints, or any biological data.
  • No Camera or Microphone Access: The app does not request or possess device permissions to record audio or take photos.
  • No Contacts or Filesystem Access: The app cannot view your device address book, call logs, SMS, or private photo galleries.

3. Local-First On-Device Storage Architecture

All educational progress and creative work remain completely private to your family. Kizmo Kids operates on a local-first architecture:

How Your Child's Data Stays Private: All educational tracking (such as completed alphabet letters, math problem scores, unlocked stickers, and drawings created in Creativity Studio) is saved locally on the device in private, sandboxed app storage via AsyncStorage. We do not operate a remote user database, analytics backend, or cloud database.
  • Child Profiles: Stored locally. Parents can create, edit, or delete child nicknames and avatar choices directly in the Parent Area.
  • Learning Milestones: Tracing progress, quiz stars, and lesson completions are saved on the phone or tablet.
  • Art & Drawings: Canvas drawings and coloring pages created by your child stay entirely within the app's local memory cache. They are never uploaded or shared.

4. Advertising Practices & Google Play Families Policy

To make foundational early childhood education freely accessible to families worldwide, the free tier of Kizmo Kids includes child-safe advertising delivered through the certified Google AdMob SDK in strict compliance with Google Play's Families Policy:

Ad Compliance Standard Implementation in Kizmo Kids Codebase
Child-Directed Treatment Every ad request is flagged with tagForChildDirectedTreatment: true and tagForUnderAgeOfConsent: true.
Maturity Rating Filter The maximum ad content maturity rating is locked to G (General Audiences suitable for young children).
No Behavioral Profiling Personalized advertising, interest-based tracking, and cross-app profiling are permanently disabled.
Conservative Banner Placement Ads are shown only on a non-intrusive static banner located at the bottom of the main menu. Ads are strictly prohibited during interactive gameplay, stories, drawing canvas, and math exercises.
Zero Disruptive Formats There are NO launch popups, NO unskippable interstitials, and NO deceptive countdown walls.

Parents who prefer a 100% ad-free experience can upgrade to Kizmo Kids Premium, which immediately suppresses all advertising across all child profiles on the device.

5. Parental Controls & Adult Mental Math Gate

To safeguard children from unintended actions, Kizmo Kids features an adult verification barrier:

  • Protected Parent Area: All adult configuration screens—including screen time reminders, sound toggles, data reset, and optional subscription management—are locked behind a dynamic mental math equation (e.g., "What is 14 × 3?").
  • Anti-Brute Force Protection: Repeated incorrect attempts temporarily lock access for 30 seconds to prevent randomized guessing by little fingers.
  • No In-App Purchases by Children: Store purchases can only be initiated from inside the parent-gated area.

6. In-App Purchases & Google Play Billing

Kizmo Kids offers an optional family subscription (Kizmo Kids Premium):

  • All financial transactions are handled exclusively and securely by Google Play Billing.
  • We never receive, process, or store credit card numbers, bank details, or billing addresses.
  • The app only receives anonymous validation tokens from Google Play confirming active subscription entitlements.
  • Subscriptions can be managed or canceled at any time by the parent through the Google Play Store app under Payments & Subscriptions.

8. Parental Rights, Data Access & Data Deletion
