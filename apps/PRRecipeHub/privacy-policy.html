Privacy Policy for PR Recipe Hub

Last Updated: September 2026 | Effective Date: September 2026

At RG Infotech Solutions (accessible via rginfotechsolutions.com) and PR Recipe Hub (prrecipehub.com), founded by Ravi Gauswami (Gausai), we respect and safeguard the personal privacy of all visitors and application users. This Privacy Policy details our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when using our Android application and website.

1. Information We Collect

While using PR Recipe Hub, we may collect the following data types to personalize recipe recommendations:

  • User Profile Data (Optional): Name, email address, dietary preferences (e.g., vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free), and saved recipe bookmarks when an account is registered.
  • Recipe Interactions: Bookmarks, created collections, search queries, and cooking timers utilized.
  • Log and Diagnostic Data: IP address, device type, browser/OS version, crash traces, and timestamp of requests to keep the server running smoothly.

2. Permissions Requested on Android Devices

PR Recipe Hub adheres to minimal permission principles on Google Play:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to fetch recipe updates, search catalogs, and sync bookmarks with the cloud backend.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to detect offline mode and switch seamlessly to locally cached recipe steps.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Optional): Used solely to ring recipe timers and notify users of newly featured culinary masterclasses.

3. Third-Party Integrations & Analytics

To analyze performance and maintain high availability, we may integrate third-party tools:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics

4. Data Protection & Confidentiality

We do NOT sell, license, or trade your personal information. Transmission of all data between the PR Recipe Hub app, prrecipehub.com, and our backend APIs is encrypted via TLS 1.3.

5. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA / DPDP)

You may at any time:

  • Access, modify, or export your saved bookmarks and recipes;
  • Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting privacy@prrecipehub.com;
  • Opt out of push notifications directly via Android system settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
