COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Direct Source-to-Target Pedagogy" mean?

Traditional language apps force every user to learn via English explanations. ShabdaKit teaches you directly from your mother tongue — for example, explaining English grammar nuances through Gujarati or Hindi mental models, making acquisition vastly faster and more natural.

Are my voice recordings uploaded to the cloud?

No. Your voice rehearsals never leave your device. The audio is captured transiently in a sandboxed cache folder to allow instant comparison against native audio models, and is erased when you leave the screen.

Does ShabdaKit work without an internet connection?

Yes. Core lessons, vocabulary flashcards, interactive quizzes, and pronunciation models work 100% offline. You can study on an airplane, during commutes, or in low-network regions.

Is ShabdaKit free to use?

Yes. ShabdaKit is completely free with zero subscription paywalls and zero third-party advertising. All curriculum features are unlocked for all learners.
