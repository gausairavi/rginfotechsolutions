Data Deletion & Privacy Sovereignty

Instructions for instant, autonomous erasure of learning data, vocabulary records, mistake notebooks, and audio cache in ShabdaKit.

Zero Cloud Storage = Zero Waiting Period

Because ShabdaKit stores your entire educational journey locally on your personal device, you never need to submit an erasure request, wait 30 days, or verify your identity to delete your records. You maintain full, immediate control directly on your device.

1
One-Tap In-App Data Wipe ("Reset All Data")

ShabdaKit provides an atomic in-app wipe feature that immediately purges your entire local learning profile, study history, flashcards, and settings:

  1. Open ShabdaKit on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings icon in the navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to App Settings.
  4. Tap "Reset All Data".
  5. Confirm the deletion prompt.

Result: All local database tables, lesson completions, quiz response records, spaced repetition intervals, Mistake Notebook entries, and cached files are permanently erased instantly.

2
Clear App Storage at the Operating System Level

You can also clear all application storage through your device's operating system settings:

  • On Android: Open Settings → Apps → ShabdaKit → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage (or "Clear Data").
  • On iOS: Open Settings → General → iPhone Storage → ShabdaKit → Delete App.

Result: The operating system completely clears the application container, resetting ShabdaKit to a fresh, initial install state.

3
Uninstalling ShabdaKit

Because all application state resides exclusively inside your personal device's sandboxed storage, removing the application immediately and irreversibly destroys all local database records, preferences, and temporary audio files.

No residual traces remain on any external server, cloud backup, or remote storage.

4
Ephemeral Voice Recordings Lifecycle

When you rehearse speaking in the Speaking & Pronunciation Rehearsal Studio:

  • Audio recordings are written exclusively to temporary cache memory.
  • They are never uploaded or streamed to any remote Speech-to-Text service or server.
  • Audio recordings are automatically purged from disk as soon as you finish or leave the rehearsal exercise.
