1. Introduction & Privacy Principles

ShabdaKit ("we", "our", or "us"), published by RG Infotech Solutions, is an advanced multilingual communication and language learning platform. We believe that personal educational progress must be private by default.

Unlike cloud-centric language services that stream every quiz answer, keystroke, and audio snippet to remote servers to build monetization profiles, ShabdaKit is built with a privacy-by-design architecture. Your learning history remains localized within your personal device.

2. Information Handled Strictly on Your Device

All information necessary to provide your personalized learning curriculum is stored exclusively inside your device's sandboxed local application storage:

  • Local Learner Profile: Your self-selected display name, chosen avatar icon, and interface language preferences. No real identity, email, or telephone number is requested or collected.
  • Curriculum Progress & Streaks: Completed modules, interactive quiz results, active study streak counters, and milestone achievement badges.
  • Vocabulary Mastery & Spaced Repetition (SM-2): Saved vocabulary cards, SuperMemo SM-2 repetition schedules, review intervals, ease factors, and retention ratings.
  • Personalized Mistake Notebook: Questions, phrases, and grammar exercises you flagged or struggled with, stored locally to power targeted revision sessions.
  • Adaptive Practice Preferences: Your customized daily commitment goal (5, 10, 15, 20, or 30 minutes) and preferred study schedule.
Absolute Isolation: None of this curriculum progress, mistake history, or vocabulary data is ever transmitted across the internet to our servers or third-party databases.

3. Microphone Usage & Ephemeral Voice Recordings

ShabdaKit includes an interactive Speaking & Pronunciation Rehearsal Studio that allows you to listen to native audio models, record your own voice, and compare pronunciation side by side:

  • Just-In-Time Permission: Microphone access (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO / NSMicrophoneUsageDescription) is requested strictly when you tap the "Record" button in a speaking rehearsal exercise. We never activate the microphone in the background.
  • Ephemeral & Sandboxed: Audio recordings are captured transiently in your device's temporary cache directory.
  • Zero Cloud Upload: Your voice recordings are never sent to external Speech-to-Text APIs, cloud servers, or linguistic data aggregators. Acoustic waveform comparison is computed entirely on-device.
  • Automatic Disk Deletion: Voice recordings are automatically unlinked and permanently erased from storage when you exit the rehearsal exercise or trigger a data wipe.

4. Local Notifications & Reminders

If you elect to configure daily practice reminders:

  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your device using native system alarm managers (expo-notifications).
  • No push notification device tokens (APNs or FCM) are ever uploaded to remote servers or push delivery intermediaries.
  • You can modify scheduled times or disable reminder notifications at any time directly in the app settings or via system application settings.

5. Data Backup, Export & Portability

To ensure total sovereignty over your learning investments, ShabdaKit provides a user-initiated local backup utility:

  • You can export your complete learning journey into a portable JSON backup file protected by an FNV-1a integrity checksum.
  • The backup file is created locally on your hardware and shared via the native system share sheet (expo-sharing).
  • You maintain full control over the storage destination (e.g. device storage, private drive, or direct transfer). ShabdaKit maintains zero remote access to your exported backups.

6. Third-Party Services, SDKs & Zero Tracking

To ensure strict compliance with Google Play Data Safety standards and Apple App Store Privacy rules:

  • Zero Advertising Networks: ShabdaKit contains no third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob or Unity Ads). Your learning environment is completely distraction-free.
  • Zero Behavioral Analytics Trackers: We do not integrate Firebase Analytics, Meta Pixel, Mixpanel, or user attribution telemetry SDKs.
  • No Cross-App Tracking: Under Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, ShabdaKit declares NSPrivacyTracking: false. We do not track you across other applications or websites.
  • Zero Tracking Cookies: The app does not set tracking cookies or collect Google Advertising Identifiers (GAID) or Apple IDFA.

7. User Data Sovereignty & Instant Data Wipe

Because your records reside entirely on your personal device, you have immediate, unilateral control over their lifecycle:

  • One-Tap In-App Data Wipe: Under Settings → App Settings → "Reset All Data", you can permanently erase all local progress, flashcards, mistake notebooks, and cached recordings in a single instant operation.
  • Operating System Storage Clear: You can purge all application state at the OS level via Android Settings → Apps → ShabdaKit → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage.
  • Application Uninstallation: Deleting ShabdaKit from your device automatically destroys the application sandbox and removes all stored learning records permanently.

8. Children's Privacy (Ages 13+ Target Audience)

ShabdaKit is designed for high school students, university learners, working professionals, and adult learners (Ages 13 and older). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13 years of age, in accordance with COPPA, GDPR-K, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA).
